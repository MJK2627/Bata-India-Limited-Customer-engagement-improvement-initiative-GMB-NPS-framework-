# Improving Local Online Presence & NPS: A Retail Summer Internship

**Summer internship · Bata India Ltd · Kochi, Kerala**
Author: Mijil K O P

A hands-on framework to improve retail store's **Google Business Profile (GBP / "GMB") visibility and ratings** and its **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**, with a 1-month progress report against baseline KPIs.



## The problem

| Area | What I found at the start |
|---|---|
| **Digital visibility** | Only 55 Google reviews, versus 1,300+ at the top-performing branch in the same city. The store ranked 4th-5th in Google results for its own location search, below nearby branches and competitors. |
| **Profile quality** | Incomplete Business Profile and an unaddressed negative photo and review sitting at the top of the listing. |
| **NPS** | Year-to-date NPS was 69. Customers often skipped the SMS survey sent at purchase, so with very few responses, 2 detractors pulled the score down noticeably. |

**Why it matters:** local search visibility drives footfall. A customer searching "shoe store near me" is shown only the top results, and a store with few reviews and an incomplete profile is effectively invisible even on a busy high street.

## Concepts in 60 seconds

- **GMB / Google Business Profile:** the free Google listing that controls how a store appears on Search and Maps (location, hours, photos, reviews).
- **NPS:** "How likely are you to recommend us?" (0-10). Promoters 9-10, Passives 7-8, Detractors 0-6. **NPS = % Promoters - % Detractors** (range -100 to +100).
- **Local Map Pack:** the top-3 map results shown for local searches. Google ranks on **Proximity, Relevance, Prominence**. The store was strong on proximity but weak on relevance (incomplete profile) and prominence (few reviews).

## Approach

### 1. Google Business Profile: three phases

1. **Technical foundation:** complete the profile (categories, attributes such as payments and accessibility), and keep Name / Address / Phone (NAP) consistent across every platform.
2. **Visuals:** upload 25+ professional, high-resolution photos (storefront, interior, products) to push down the negative image ("visual dilution"), plus weekly Google Posts with seasonal offers.
3. **Review management:** respond to 100% of reviews within 24 hours, using keyword-rich replies for local SEO, and handle the negative review as a public service-recovery opportunity.

### 2. NPS improvement

- **Set a target:** a daily goal of 5 survey entries per day, with every staff member briefed on the SMS survey.
- **Close the loop with detractors:** review every 0-6 response, contact the customer quickly, fix the problem first, and document it so it doesn't recur.
- **Create memorable moments:** a warm greeting, personalised recommendations, fitting assistance, and a genuine thank-you at checkout.


## Results after 1 month | Achivements

| KPI | Baseline | 2-month target | After 1 month | New target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google search position (store name + area) | 5th (second last) | Top 3 (Map Pack) | **Top 3** | Top 1 |
| Total Google reviews | 55 | 200 | **125** | 250 |
| Google rating | 4.1 | 4.2 | **4.6** | 4.8 |
| Search-to-visit conversion | 70% | High | **82%** | 90% |
| NPS (YTD) | 69 | 80 | **84** | 90 |

**Activity in the reporting month (April):** 245 Business Profile impressions and 201 direction requests made from the profile.

**Takeaway:** the two-month targets for search position, rating and NPS were reached within the first month, and the review count more than doubled.
